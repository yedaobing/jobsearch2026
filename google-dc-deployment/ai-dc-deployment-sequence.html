AI Data Center Deployment Sequence

The end-to-end sequence for bringing an AI data center from design to production, and where the hardware TPM sits in it. Each phase carries a scoping question to confirm with the hiring manager.

The hardware TPM's own milestones and gates (P6 and P7) are detailed in the hardware deployment workflow.

Overview

Nine phases, four levels of TPM involvement

The bands above the track show the hardware TPM's assumed role: track design and procurement, coordinate construction and commissioning, own rack install through energization row by row, then hand off to customer engineering.

Assumed scope

Coordinate construction, gray space and white space fit-out with facilities, the GC and other TPMs, up to the busway and the rack drop.

Owned scope

Deliver hardware row by row, from facility handover through rack energization and POST.

After handoff

Customer engineering runs burn-in, validation and platform bring-up on each energized row.

Across phases

General scoping questions

Scale

  1. How many sites and builds would I cover at the same time?
  2. How many racks or rows per build, and what rack types?

Team and tools

  1. Which EMs and TPMs would I work with most?
  2. Which tracking tools and reporting cadence do they use today?

Success

  1. What does success look like in the first 90 days?
  2. Where have past builds slipped most?
