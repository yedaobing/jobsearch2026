Data Center Program Workflow

Butlr Senior PM, Data Operations: one owner from first customer call to fleet
Gate: must pass to enter next phase ↺ Failed gate loops back
Grows in steps — phases 2–5 repeat at each step POC about 6 racks and PDUs Expansion about 5 more sets Full site or fleet
Phase 1 · before the deal
Scope
  • Work out requirements with the customer
  • Match to what hardware and firmware can deliver
  • Size the POC: units, form factor, success criteria
  • Hand software needs to software PM
At scale: reference designs make intake repeatable
G1
POC scoped
Success criteria and kit list agreed
Phase 2
Plan and prepare
  • Kick off; set owners, timeline, cadence
  • Build, test, and QA the kit with hardware team
  • Ship from Burlingame stock
  • Confirm site access, install window, Hive connection
At scale: ship from Shenzhen; customs for Asia sites
G2
Ready to install
Kit tested and on site; window confirmed
Phase 3
Pilot and validate
  • Mount over busbars; face PDUs and batteries
  • Check readings against a FLIR scan
  • Tune placement, thresholds, firmware
  • Log product gaps for hardware and firmware
At scale: in-rack sensor, built or with rack makers
G3
Validation passed
Hotspots caught; alerts accurate; test report
Phase 4
Accept and hand over
  • Customer sees data in Butlr dashboard or API
  • Confirm alerts, docs, and support path
  • Close punch list with owners and dates
  • Get customer sign-off
At scale: feed customer BMC and telemetry tools
G4
Customer accepts
Data live in Butlr tools; sign-off received
Phase 5
Operate and expand
  • Lead technical customer success
  • Watch sensor health and batteries
  • Run check-ins; resolve issues fast
  • Scope the next step with Sales
At scale: hand off to customer success team
G5
Next step ordered
Expansion or fleet order signed
Every week, all phases Prioritize the POC pipeline  ·  Track the plan  ·  Raise risks early  ·  Feed field lessons to Product  ·  Report status
